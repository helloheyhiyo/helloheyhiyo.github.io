Privacy

Last updated 4 November 2026

The short version. If you join the list, we keep your email to send you news about Tiny Crisis. We count visits anonymously, without cookies. The name you type on the box stays on your phone. We never sell anything about you. Want out? Use "unsubscribe" in any email, or write to us.

Who we are

Tiny Crisis is a baby shower gift brand being set up in Dubai, UAE. Our trade licence is in progress; this page will show the licensed business name and number as soon as it is issued. Contact: hello@thetinycrisis.com.

1. The list (your email)

  • What: the email address you type in the form.
  • Why: to send you the first look at the boxes and the odd update. We write rarely.
  • Where: stored by MailerLite, the service that sends our emails.
  • How long: until you leave the list. Every email has an unsubscribe link; you can also email us and we delete you.

2. Visit counts (no cookies)

  • What: which steps of the page were reached (for example "the box was spun" or "the form was tapped"), the page address, the website you came from (its name only), and your screen width.
  • Not included: your name, your email, the name you type on the box.
  • How: a random code saved in your browser's local storage (not a cookie) so a visit isn't counted twice. Counts are processed by PostHog in the United States.
  • To stop it: clear this site's data in your browser settings.

3. The name on the box

The name you type to see it on the box is shown on your screen only. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

4. What we don't do

No advertising pixels, no selling or renting of your details, no photos of babies.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you at hello@thetinycrisis.com. We reply within 7 days. We follow the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021).

6. When the shop opens

Ordering is not open yet and this site takes no payments. Before it opens, this page will be updated to cover orders, delivery details and payments.
